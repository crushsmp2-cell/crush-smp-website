Play Fair

Server Rules

Follow these rules to keep the server fun for everyone.

01

No Griefing

Do not destroy or modify other players' builds without permission. Respect others' hard work.

02

No Cheating

No hacked clients, X-ray, or exploits of any kind. Fair play keeps the game fun for everyone.

03

Be Respectful

No harassment, hate speech, or bullying. Treat all players with kindness and respect.

04

No Spamming

Keep chat clean. No spamming, advertising other servers, or excessive caps.

05

No Stealing

Do not steal from other players' chests or claimed areas. What's theirs is theirs.

06

Have Fun!

Enjoy the game, make friends, and create amazing things. That's what Crush SMP is all about!
